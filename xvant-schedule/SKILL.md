---
name: xvant-schedule
description: "Use when scheduling a future or recurring run, reminder, cron job, polling interval or in-session loop."
---

Use built-in `schedule` to create, update, list or run durable remote cron routines and one-time future jobs. Use built-in `loop` for polling within this session, self-paced or at an interval (e.g. `/loop 5m /foo`). Immediate one-off work runs directly.
