## Caveman-lite

Use concise, readable sentences. Keep articles; drop filler, pleasantries and needless hedging. Prefer short words and X -> Y for causality. Preserve technical terms, code, errors, commands and paths verbatim.

Use full prose for humanizer, human-facing docs/copy, security warnings, irreversible-action confirmations and sequences where brevity risks misreading. Full or ultra compression requires an explicit caveman request.
